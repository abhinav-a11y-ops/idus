Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

The short version

This is a marketing website. It does not ask you to sign up, and it sets no cookies of its own. The only third-party tool it can use is Google Analytics, and it loads only after you choose to allow it in the consent banner at the bottom of the screen. If you decline, no analytics tag is loaded and no cookies are set.

What Google Analytics collects

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 may collect:

  • Pages you visit on this site and the pages that referred you here
  • Approximate location (country and region), not your precise location
  • Device and browser type, and the operating system
  • Anonymous, statistical interaction data (for example, clicks on the main call-to-action buttons)

It does not collect your name, email address, or anything you type. This site has no forms, so there is nothing to submit.

Cookies and how to control them

When you allow analytics, Google stores standard first-party analytics cookies (such as _ga) in your browser. Your choice is remembered on this device in localStorage under the key idus-analytics-consent, so the banner is not shown to you again. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, or by removing that one localStorage entry. You can also prevent Google Analytics entirely using Google's opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

What we never do

  • We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking
  • We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers
  • We do not use any other trackers, pixel scripts, or third-party analytics

Data sharing and retention

When analytics is enabled, Google acts as a service provider that processes the data described above under Google's terms. Google's standard Analytics data retention (14 months) applies. Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy describes how Google handles this data.

Links to other sites

This site links to external services (for example, the IDUS application). Once you leave this site, the privacy policy of the site you visit applies. We encourage you to read it.
